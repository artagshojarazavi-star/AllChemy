About these results
  • Formula and equation balancing are exact calculations (integer atom/charge bookkeeping). Molar masses use rounded standard atomic weights from the built-in element table.
  • Product prediction (reactants without an arrow) is a rule-based heuristic. It is not experimentally verified and gives no enthalpy, rate or observation data yet.
  • Structures are drawn from the SMILES you type. Bridged / cage ring systems may be drawn distorted.
  • Works fully offline. No data leaves the device.
